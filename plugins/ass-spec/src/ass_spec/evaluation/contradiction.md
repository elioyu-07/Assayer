## Functional Requirements

FR-201 cancels a submitted request.
AC-201 requires an observable cancellation result.
CASE-201 covers the normal path, boundary input, invalid input, access denial,
timeout, retry, and recovery behavior.

Out of Scope: cancellation after settlement.

Revision 1.0.0 established this baseline.














FR-202: A requester might cancel a submitted request.
FR-203: Only an approver might cancel a submitted request.
